# Task 7: Reflection and reuse

**1. What I changed my mind about.** I expected duplicate messages, the owners' top complaint, to cost sign-ups. The data didn't show it: 1 of 38 leads who got a duplicate bought (2.6%), against 7 of 212 who didn't (3.3%): too few to tell apart. So I framed it as an owner-trust problem, not a revenue one; it still matters with 11 renewals in March.

**2. What I'd hand the next engineer.** A starter kit in the [repo](https://github.com/sami-bre/trp2-delivery-challenge):
- A first-week checklist: define every dashboard number, trace the funnel to the paid event, and test every rule that must reach a human.
- A reusable eval: code for clear rules, an AI judge run three times for judgment calls, and a hand check of every fail. The judge and I each caught a mistake the other missed.
- A control-model test, to tell a model problem from an instructions problem.
